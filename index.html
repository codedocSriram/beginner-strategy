Frequently Asked Questions

Can a complete beginner attend the 90-Minute Masterclass?

Absolutely. The masterclass is designed to cover foundational concepts in a clear, accessible manner before diving into strategy. While some basic knowledge helps, Deepak will ensure all beginners can follow the core blueprint presented.

Is this masterclass only focused on Options Trading?

Yes, the primary focus is on high-probability index options trading (Nifty/Bank Nifty), which is highly relevant in the Indian markets. However, the core risk management and technical analysis principles are applicable across stocks and commodities as well.

What technical tools do I need to attend the session?

You only need a stable internet connection and access to Zoom (desktop or mobile app). While we discuss strategies using standard charts, you do not need any paid software to benefit from the content.

Will a recording of the masterclass be provided later?

To encourage live interaction and Q&A, no public recording is guaranteed. We highly recommend attending live to get your doubts cleared and ensure you don't miss the time-sensitive strategies shared during the session.

Is this a free workshop or is there a hidden charge?

The 90-Minute Live Masterclass is completely free of charge. Our goal is to provide immense value upfront. There are no hidden fees. We will briefly mention our advanced paid courses (like the Weekend Batch) at the end, but the main content is yours free.
